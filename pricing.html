FAQ

Pricing questions

Is the tool really free?

Yes. The full tool — rate table, calculators, complete earnings audit, claim export and alerts — is free to use with no paywall. That's the plan, permanently.

What does the free plan include?

Everything: the rate table, commission and break-even calculators, the full earnings audit with all flagged line items, the claim report export, rate-change alerts, the URL submitter and the backlink directory.

What do I get for paying?

The Supporter plans add perks: no ads anywhere, priority email support, and early access to new tools. They don't gate any features — every tool stays free. Paying supports the project.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Email admin@amzloss.com or manage through Paystack. You'll keep your Supporter perks until the end of the paid period.

Is the 14-day refund real?

Yes. Contact us within 14 days of your first charge and we'll refund it, no questions asked.

Is my earnings data ever uploaded?

No. The audit runs in your browser and your file never leaves your device — on any plan.

Which currency will I be charged in?

Plans are priced and charged in Nigerian Naira (NGN). Paystack shows the exact charge in Naira before you confirm.
