Data Deletion

How to remove your data from AmzLoss — and why there usually isn't any.

What we store

Almost nothing. Every tool on AmzLoss runs entirely in your browser. Your earnings reports, URLs, tracking IDs and API keys never leave your device and are never uploaded to us.

Browser-stored data (you control it)

  • Theme & language preference — clear your browser storage for amzloss.com to reset.
  • Open PageRank API key (Link Tools) — click "Save" with an empty field, or clear site storage.
  • URL submitter progress — resets when you clear site storage.

Server-side data

If you subscribed as a Supporter, Paystack holds your email and billing relationship (we can see only the email used for receipts). To delete it:

  1. Email admin@amzloss.com with subject "Data deletion request", or
  2. Cancel via Paystack and email us to purge our records.

We action verified requests within 7 days and confirm by reply. Third-party processors (Paystack, Mailchimp) have their own deletion processes which we will initiate on your behalf where applicable.

🔒No accounts, no tracking database. There is no AmzLoss profile to delete because none exists.
