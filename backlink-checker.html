Backlink maintenance

Why you should check your backlinks regularly

Links disappear. A site redesign, a cleanup plugin, or a page moving to a new URL can silently drop the backlink you earned. A backlink that vanishes loses you both traffic and the link equity that supports your rankings. Run this free backlink checker monthly on every site that lists you — paste the page URL and your domain name, and it scans the live HTML for your link. If it's gone, ask the site owner to restore it, or re-submit and rebuild the relationship.

To measure the whole picture — not just one page but every page on the internet — use the backlink counter above. Paste any domain and it reports the total number of backlinks pointing at it, its global rank, and its authority score from the Open PageRank index.
