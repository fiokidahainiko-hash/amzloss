About AmzLoss

The independent check on Amazon Associates payouts.

Why AmzLoss exists

In 2026, Amazon cut affiliate commissions by up to 50% in some categories and quietly reduced the reporting detail affiliates rely on to verify their earnings. Rate changes arrive with no reliable public changelog. Item-level reporting was reduced. Affiliates were left asking the same question:

"Am I actually being paid what I earned?"

Most can't answer it — and a wrong answer costs real money every month.

What we believe

  • Your data is your data. That's why AmzLoss processes your earnings report entirely in your browser. We never upload, store, or see a single line of it.
  • Trust is earned with math. We label results as "possible irregularities," never "guaranteed claims," so returns and ambiguous rows are never falsely blamed.
  • Independent means independent. AmzLoss is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a partner of Amazon or Amazon Associates.

Who it's for

Bloggers, YouTubers, influencers, and niche-site owners earning through Amazon Associates — from small hobby sites to large multi-site portfolios.

Who runs AmzLoss

AmzLoss is built and run by Fiokidaha Usen Iniko, based in Uyo, Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria. It is an independent, one-person project — no corporate shell, no hidden team, just someone who watched fellow affiliates lose income to the 2026 commission cuts and wanted a tool that actually checked the numbers.

You can reach the founder directly at admin@amzloss.com. If you email us, a real person reads it and replies — usually within one business day.

Our approach

The tools are free and fully functional. We keep the lights on two ways: a small amount of advertising on the free plan, and optional paid plans that simply remove the ads. No feature is ever locked behind a paywall.
