Your URL was pinged. Now what?

A ping tells engines your page exists and asks them to crawl it. Indexing still takes time — here's what to expect and do next.

  • Check each engine's crawler report in 1–3 days — Bing Webmaster, Yandex Webmaster, Seznam and Naver all log IndexNow activity under "URL Submission" or "Crawl". Look for "discovered" or "200 OK" to confirm it was fetched.
  • Confirm your page is reachable — the URL must return HTTP 200 with no redirect loop or robots.txt block, or engines will discard the ping.
  • Verify the page is canonical — one page = one URL. If your site serves duplicate URLs, pick the canonical and ping only that one.
  • Re-ping after major edits — IndexNow is designed for changed content. Update the page, then auto-submit again so engines re-crawl the new version.
  • Google won't appear from this — Google doesn't use IndexNow. Add the page in Google Search Console (the manual card below) or request indexing from URL Inspection.
  • Don't spam it — re-pinging unchanged URLs can get your key rate-limited (HTTP 429). Ping on publish or real edits, not daily.
