Commission cuts quietly shrink your income

On $5,000 of monthly revenue, that's $200 gone every month, or $2,400 a year. Amazon commission math

How commission-rate changes affect affiliates

Amazon Associates pays a percentage of the eligible order amount (product price minus gift-wrap, shipping, and certain taxes). When Amazon changes that rate — which it did across most categories in 2026 — your earnings on the same traffic change instantly. Use this calculator alongside the 2026 commission rate table and the commission calculator to see both the impact of rate changes and your estimated earnings at current rates.

Three things to know about commission changes:

  • Rate changes apply per order date — orders placed before a change are paid at the old rate, even if the payout arrives later.
  • Your category mix matters — if most of your traffic is in a cut category, the impact is proportionally larger.
  • The audit catches discrepancies — if your earnings report shows a rate that doesn't match what was published on the order date, the earnings audit flags it.
Frequently asked

Commission loss calculator questions

How do I find my previous and current Amazon commission rates?

Check the AmzLoss Commission Rates page for the 2026 rate table with pre-cut and current rates by category. You can also look at your Amazon Associates dashboard under the Rate Changes section, or check your earnings report — the rate column shows the rate that applied on each order date.

Why is the commission loss different from what Amazon shows?

This calculator estimates the pure rate-change impact on your revenue. Your actual earnings also depend on order volume, product mix, returns, and which rates applied on specific order dates. The AmzLoss Earnings Audit can verify exactly what you were paid versus the applicable rate.

What does "eligible order amount" mean?

Amazon excludes gift-wrap fees, shipping charges, and certain taxes from the commission base. The "eligible order amount" is the product price minus those exclusions — it is the number your commission rate is applied to.

How is this different from the commission calculator?

The Commission Calculator shows what you earn at a given rate. This tool compares two rates and tells you how much the difference costs you in monthly and annual revenue.

Should I restructure my content around the higher-paying categories?

It depends on your niche and audience. If you have flexibility, creating content around categories with higher commission rates means more revenue per sale. But don't abandon categories that convert well — a higher-converting 4% category can still outperform a lower-converting 10% one.
