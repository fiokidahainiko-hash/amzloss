Why this matters

The same product pays differently on every network

A fashion item might pay 12% through ShareASale, 8% through CJ or Impact, and 3.5% through eBay Partner Network. On 100 sales of $80, that's the difference between $960 and $280 a month — for the same traffic. The calculator shows you, per network and niche, where your audience is worth the most.

FAQ

Network calculator questions

Which affiliate network pays the most?

Software/SaaS programs typically pay the highest at 15–25%, followed by fashion, beauty and pets at 8–12%. Electronics and grocery pay the least. Pick the network strong in your niche — the headline rate means nothing if the merchants you want aren't there.

Do these rates apply to my account?

No. They're typical advisory ranges so you can compare networks side by side. Every merchant sets its own rate, which can differ by program tier, and can change at any time. Confirm in the network's program terms before you invest time.

Can I run multiple networks at once?

Yes — many publishers do. Some run one network per niche; others keep several for redundancy. Impact and Awin give you many merchants in one dashboard, which is handy when starting out.

How is this different from the Amazon calculator?

The Amazon calculator compares 2026 Amazon Associates rates against pre-cut rates. This tool covers the independent networks so you can see, for your niche, which platform pays best industry-wide.
