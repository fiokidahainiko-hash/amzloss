FAQ

Directory questions

Is the backlink free?

Yes. Listing is free and there's no paid placement. We keep it free to keep the directory genuinely useful — the same ethos as our audit tool.

How long does approval take?

There's no manual wait. Add the verification snippet to your site, click Verify my site, and once we detect it you're listed immediately — with a backlink.

Do I need to link back to AmzLoss?

Yes — that's the deal. The verification snippet you paste contains a link back to our directory, plus your unique code. We check the code is really on your site before we list you, so nobody gets a backlink without actually linking back.

What if the snippet isn't detected?

Make sure it's pasted into the HTML of the exact URL you submitted, saved and deployed — then click verify again. Some sites block automatic checks; in that case contact us at admin@amzloss.com and we'll verify manually.

Does listing guarantee my site ranks higher?

No — no single directory link guarantees rankings. A relevant link like this is one small, natural signal among many. Focus on good content first.
