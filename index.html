Questions, answered

Is my Amazon earnings data safe?

Yes. Everything is processed in your browser. We never upload, store, or see your numbers.

Does "irregularity" mean I was definitely underpaid?

No. We flag possible irregularities with a plain-language reason. Returns, bounties and ambiguous rows are separated out so they're never falsely flagged against you.

How is this different from a calculator?

Calculators show a rate and multiply it. AmzLoss compares what you were actually paid against what the rate should have earned you — and spots the difference.

Right now, yes — the entire tool is unlocked during the launch period (until September 10, 2026). After that, free keeps the summary, worst categories, and top flagged items; paid unlocks the full list and claim export and removes the ads.
