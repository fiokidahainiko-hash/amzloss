FAQ

Questions, answered

Is my data safe with AmzLoss?

Yes. Everything is processed in your browser. We never upload, store, or see your earnings, links or URLs.

Are these tools only for Amazon affiliates?

No. The audit, rate table, calculator and break-even tool focus on Amazon because that's where underpayments are hardest to verify, but the network calculator, link tools, URL submitter and backlink directory work for any site and any network.

Does "irregularity" mean I was definitely underpaid?

No. We flag possible irregularities with a plain-language reason. Returns, bounties and ambiguous rows are separated out so they're never falsely flagged against you.

Is the free plan really the full toolkit?

Yes. The entire toolkit — rate table, calculators, full audit, claim export and alerts — is free to use forever. Paid Supporter plans are optional extras like ad-free browsing, priority support and early access.

Why is the URL submitter free?

Every platform on the list offers free submissions — the tool just saves you the time of opening them all. It's a free backlink and indexing generator, and it never sends your URL to any server itself.
