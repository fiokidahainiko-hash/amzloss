Privacy & Data Promise

Your earnings data never leaves your browser

The AmzLoss audit runs entirely on your device. We do not upload, store, or see your Amazon earnings, order values, or report contents — on any plan, free or paid.

What we collect

Only what you choose to give us: an email address if you subscribe, and payment details handled by Paystack (never by us). Optional anonymous benchmarks are off by default and contain no names or amounts.

Ads & analytics

The free plan is ad-supported. Ad and analytics partners may use cookies; you can manage these in your browser. Paid plans remove the ads.

Payments

Card details are processed by Paystack, a PCI-DSS compliant provider. AmzLoss never sees or stores your card number.

Your rights

You can delete your account and data at any time. Email admin@amzloss.com and we'll action it within 30 days.

Independent

AmzLoss is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a partner of Amazon or Amazon Associates.
