Privacy & Data Promise

Your earnings data never leaves your browser

The AmzLoss audit runs entirely on your device. We do not upload, store, or see your Amazon earnings, order values, or report contents — on any plan, free or paid.

What we collect

Only what you choose to give us: an email address if you subscribe, and payment details handled by Paystack (never by us). Optional anonymous benchmarks are off by default and contain no names or amounts. We do not require an account to use any tool.

Cookies & analytics

The free plan is ad-supported. We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate usage (pages visited, approximate region, device type) and Google AdSense to serve ads. These partners may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies; you can manage or disable them in your browser settings or via Google's Ad Settings. Paid plans remove the ads.

Google API services

AmzLoss uses Google's YouTube Data API to publish short video summaries of new blog posts. AmzLoss's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use the API only to upload our own original videos to the owner's channel. We do not read, modify, or disclose any other user's Google data.

Third-party sharing

We never sell or rent your personal data. The only third parties that receive data are: (1) Paystack, which processes card payments and is PCI-DSS compliant, and (2) our ad and analytics partners (Google Analytics, Google AdSense), which receive standard usage signals. AmzLoss never sees or stores your card number.

Data retention

Usage analytics are retained by our analytics provider for their standard retention period and are not linked to your identity by us. If you email us or subscribe, we keep your contact details only until you ask us to delete them.

Children's privacy

AmzLoss is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

Payments

Card details are processed by Paystack, a PCI-DSS compliant provider. AmzLoss never sees or stores your card number.

Your rights

You can delete your account and data at any time. Email admin@amzloss.com and we'll action it within 30 days. You can also request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be noted on this page with an updated date.
