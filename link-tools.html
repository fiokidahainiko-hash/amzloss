Link Tools

Two free, browser-only tools that work with the AmzLoss directory: build clean Amazon affiliate links with your tracking tag, and check whether a site still carries your backlink.

Tool 1 · Link builder

Amazon Affiliate Link Builder

Paste any Amazon product URL, add your tracking ID, and get a clean affiliate link with a copy-paste snippet for your blog.

Works with amazon.com, amazon.co.uk, amazon.de and other Amazon marketplaces. Everything runs in your browser — nothing is sent to a server.

Your links

The snippet includes rel="nofollow sponsored" — the FTC and Amazon both require sponsored links to be disclosed.

Tool 2 · Backlink checker

Backlink Checker

Paste a site URL and your link (or a keyword like your domain name) to see whether the page still contains your backlink. Uses a public CORS proxy — no sign-up.

Some sites block automated requests. If a check fails, open the page in your browser and search for your keyword to confirm manually.

Result

Enter a URL and keyword to check.

Why it matters

Links are how a niche site grows

Every affiliate site runs on two loops: traffic (which products and deals you promote) and links (the reputation that makes rankings stick). The AmzLoss backlink directory gives you a free, relevant starting point; these tools keep your links clean and verifiable. Together they're a small but honest link-building kit — no spam, no paid link schemes.

🔒Private by design. Both tools run entirely in your browser. The backlink checker fetches pages through a public CORS proxy, but your tracking ID and links never leave your device or get stored.
