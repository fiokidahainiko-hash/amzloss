Affiliate link best practices

Clean links convert better and keep you compliant

Every Amazon Associates link should include your tracking ID (tag=) so clicks and purchases are credited to you — publishing a bare product URL earns nothing. This Amazon affiliate link builder strips old tracking parameters and appends your tag in one step. It also marks links rel="nofollow sponsored", which both Amazon's operating agreement and the FTC require when you get paid to recommend a product. The same hygiene applies across networks: build a network calculator strategy for ShareASale, CJ, Impact or Awin, then create clean, tagged links for each merchant.

Private by design. All three tools run entirely in your browser. The backlink checker fetches pages through a public CORS proxy and the counter reads the public Open PageRank API — but your tracking ID, keywords and API key never leave your device or get stored.
Backlink maintenance

Why you should check your backlinks regularly

Links disappear. A site redesign, a cleanup plugin, or a page moving to a new URL can silently drop the directory backlink you earned. A backlink that vanishes loses you both traffic and the link equity that supports your rankings. Run this free backlink checker monthly on every site that lists you — paste the page URL and your domain name, and it scans the live HTML for your link. If it's gone, ask the site owner to restore it, or re-submit and rebuild the relationship.

To measure the whole picture — not just one page but every page on the internet — use the backlink counter above it. Paste any domain and it reports the total number of backlinks pointing at it, its global rank, and its authority score from the Open PageRank index. Track your own domain's count as you build links, and snoop on competitors to see whose link-building is working. It's the same "number of backlinks" stat paid SEO tools charge for, free here.
