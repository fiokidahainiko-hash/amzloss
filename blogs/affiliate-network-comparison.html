Affiliate Network Comparison 2026 — ShareASale, CJ, Impact, Awin & More

🌐Free tool:Network Calculator — compare Amazon vs ShareASale, CJ, Impact & Awin.

The 2026 Amazon Associates cuts changed the calculus for every affiliate. If a category you promoted dropped from 10% to 4%, the same content now earns half. Diversification isn't optional anymore — and that means joining one or more affiliate networks that aggregate hundreds of merchants under one dashboard.

But "which network?" is the question every affiliate asks, and the answer isn't one-size-fits-all. This guide compares the major networks — ShareASale, CJ Affiliate, Impact, Awin, Rakuten and eBay Partner Network — on the factors that actually matter: commission ranges, niche strength, approval difficulty, payment thresholds, and how they compare to Amazon Associates.

The big six at a glance

Before the details, the one-paragraph summary:

  • ShareASale — the balanced all-rounder. Fast approval, clean reporting, strong in lifestyle and home niches. Great first network.
  • CJ Affiliate — the giant of US retail. Huge brand roster, but steeper program approvals and a dated interface.
  • Impact — the modern one. Best dashboard and automation, growing fast, but selective approvals and higher minimums on some programs.
  • Awin — the international option. Strong in the UK/EU and with the widest merchant variety, including more SaaS.
  • Rakuten Advertising — premium US brands and luxury, selective approvals, good for fashion and travel.
  • eBay Partner Network — the Amazon-style baseline. Lower rates (roughly 1–3.5%) but easy approval and a cookie window that helps casual content.

Why networks beat betting on one program

Amazon's quiet 2026 cuts exposed the core weakness of single-program dependency: when the merchant changes the rules, your whole month changes. An affiliate network spreads that risk across dozens or hundreds of merchants. If one program drops its rate, your other programs still perform.

Networks also add variety you can't get from Amazon alone — especially recurring commissions. SaaS and subscription programs commonly pay 15–25% and often on a recurring basis, which is a different income profile entirely from a one-time product sale.

Approval difficulty

Your site is the first thing networks look at. A site with real, recent articles passes easily; a fresh domain with a homepage and nothing else gets rejected or put on hold.

  • Easiest: eBay Partner Network, ShareASale, Awin.
  • Middle: CJ Affiliate, Impact (depends heavily on the specific merchant program).
  • Hardest: Rakuten — selective publishers, and some premium programs review your traffic and content before approving.

Practical tip: apply to several networks at once, then apply to merchant programs once the network account is active. Rejections are normal — reapply after publishing more content.

Payment thresholds and methods

Minimum payout thresholds (approximate, they change):

  • ShareASale — $50 minimum, paid by check, direct deposit or ACH.
  • CJ Affiliate — $50 minimum, monthly, bank transfer or check.
  • Impact — depends on merchant payout terms; some programs have higher minimums.
  • Awin — £20/$20/€20 minimum, monthly.
  • Rakuten — varies by program; bank transfer or check.
  • eBay — $10 minimum via PayPal or direct deposit.

How to choose for your niche

There's no single "best" network — there's a best fit:

  • Fashion, home, lifestyle: ShareASale and Rakuten are strong.
  • Tech and software: Impact and Awin carry the most SaaS/SaaS-adjacent programs.
  • US retail brands: CJ Affiliate has the biggest brand roster.
  • UK/EU audience: Awin is the natural fit.
  • Broad, casual content: eBay Partner Network is the easiest entry, like a more forgiving Amazon.

The key is to compare your niche's rate across networks before you commit content — the same audience can earn 2x more on the right program. Run your numbers through the network calculator to see the difference in dollars.

Amazon Associates vs. affiliate networks

Amazon remains worth keeping: its cookie window, breadth of products and brand trust convert well, and some categories still pay decently. But after 2026, treat it as the baseline, not the business. Networks fill the gaps Amazon can't:

  • Higher rates in many niches (especially SaaS at 15–25%).
  • Recurring commissions for subscriptions.
  • Merchant choice — you're not tied to one rate table.
  • Granular reporting — no "reporting got weaker" surprises.

And keep auditing Amazon itself: run the free earnings audit periodically to catch any line-item underpayments before they stack up.

Frequently asked questions

Which affiliate network is best for beginners?

ShareASale and Awin have the fastest approvals and clearest dashboards. eBay Partner Network is the easiest to join but pays less. CJ is strong but stricter on merchant approvals.

Which affiliate network pays the highest commissions?

It's merchant-driven, not network-driven. Software and subscription programs pay 15–25% on many networks; electronics and grocery pay the least. Compare your niche with the AmzLoss network calculator.

Can I run an affiliate network alongside Amazon Associates?

Yes, and after the 2026 cuts it's the smart move. Keep Amazon for its brand and breadth, add a network strong in your niche, and audit both regularly.

Do I need a website to join?

Almost always. Networks review your site before approving program applications. A content site with real articles gets approved far more easily than a fresh domain.
