Amazon Associates Commission Rates 2026

Free tool:Current Rate Table — see the live 2026 rates by category and market.

Ask "what's the Amazon Associates commission rate?" and you'll get a misleading answer — because there has never been a single rate, and 2026 made the spread wider than ever. Some categories were cut by nearly half, a few barely moved, and several digital categories held their ground. This guide maps the 2026 rate picture so you know exactly what changed, what held, and what your content is actually earning right now.

Where rates stand after the 2026 cuts

Across the US and most major marketplaces, most categories now sit between 3% and 8%, down from a pre-2026 range that topped out around 10% for many physical goods. The shape of the market:

  • Held their rates: several digital categories — including digital books and Amazon digital services — still pay in the double digits, and some everyday categories moved little.
  • Cut but functional: home, kitchen, and everyday categories took modest reductions and remain workable for content sites.
  • Cut hardest: luxury goods, high-end furniture, automotive, and tools dropped the most — some by close to half, with luxury paying as little as 2.5%.

The exact, up-to-date numbers for each category and marketplace live in the Current Rate Table — the same source the calculator and audit use, so you never calculate against a stale number.

What the deepest cuts mean for your content

A rate cut doesn't just lower your payout — it rewrites the economics of a content plan. Before 2026, a mid-priced furniture review at 8% was a solid earner. At the post-cut rate, the same page earns roughly half per sale, and it may no longer justify the production cost. The practical response is to re-prioritize:

  • Shift toward categories that held: digital products and everyday home items now look relatively better than they did.
  • Chase order value, not rate: a 3% rate on a $400 item can out-earn a 6% rate on a $40 item for the same effort.
  • Stop quoting old percentages: any page that still says "10% commission" is promising readers a number that no longer exists. Update them or watch your click-through sink.

Marketplace differences: US vs UK

Rates are set per marketplace, and 2026 hit them unevenly. The UK and US followed broadly similar patterns — luxury and physical-goods cuts, digital held — but the exact percentages differ, and so do the affected categories. Germany, France, Italy, and Spain each have their own schedules as well. Two implications:

  • Never copy a US rate onto a UK product page; the gap can be a full percentage point or more.
  • When you run the calculator, select the marketplace the shopper will actually buy from — the tool keeps each market's rates separate.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Amazon Associates commission rate in 2026?

There is no single rate. Most categories pay between 3% and 8% depending on category and marketplace, with luxury goods as low as 2.5% and some digital categories still in double digits.

Which categories were cut the most?

Luxury goods and physical-goods categories like furniture, tools, and automotive took the deepest cuts — some by nearly half. Home and everyday categories moved less, and digital categories mostly held.

Are rates the same in the UK and US?

No. Each marketplace sets its own rates, and the 2026 changes hit markets unevenly. Always check your specific marketplace before quoting a commission.
