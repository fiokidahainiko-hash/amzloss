Affiliate link best practices

How Amazon Affiliate Tracking Links Work

Every Amazon Associates link should include your tracking ID (tag=) so clicks and purchases are credited to you — publishing a bare product URL earns nothing. This Amazon affiliate link builder strips old tracking parameters and appends your tag in one step. It also marks links rel="nofollow sponsored", which both Amazon's operating agreement and the FTC require when you get paid to recommend a product.

The same hygiene applies across networks: build a network calculator strategy for ShareASale, CJ, Impact or Awin, then create clean, tagged links for each merchant.

Private by design. The link builder runs entirely in your browser. Your tracking ID and product URLs never leave your device or get stored.
